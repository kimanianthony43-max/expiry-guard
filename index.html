⏰ REMIND ME BEFORE EXPIRY
This app reminds you BEFORE expiry - not on expiry day. Remove-by is BEFORE expiry. Example: Bakery item expires 23 Nov, Supermarket Standard Rule removes it by 21 Nov (2 days before). Long-life item expiring in 2 years is removed 45 days before.
Filters are by REMOVE-BY date, not expiry date. 3yr → 60, 2yr → 45, 1yr → 30, 6mo → 14, Bakery → 2, Daily Fresh → 1.
⚠️ This is a file app, not a Play Store app - it cannot pop up by itself at 8am. To get a daily reminder: Set a phone Alarm at 8am called "Open ExpiryGuard" - open app, tap Send All Due Alerts. That is your reminder.
