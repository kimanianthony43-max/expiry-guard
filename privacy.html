ExpiryGuard Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

ExpiryGuard helps store staff track product expiry dates.

What we collect

We do not collect, store, or sell your personal data on our servers. Items, photos, supplier names and numbers you enter are stored only on your device / browser.

WhatsApp

When you tap WhatsApp, your device opens WhatsApp with a message you wrote. We do not send WhatsApp messages automatically.

Photos

Photos you take stay on your device unless you export a backup yourself.

Backup

Export / Import Backup creates a file on your device. You control it.

Contact

For questions, contact the developer via the GitHub repository: kimarianthony43-max/expiry-guard
